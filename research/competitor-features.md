# Stress App: Competitor + Feature Research (2026-10-15)

## Competitor snapshot

| App | Price | What users love | What reviews complain about |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headspace | $12.99/mo, $69.99/yr, family $99.99 | Structured beginner programs, animations, AI companion Ebb bundled | Billing nightmares (charged in trials, no refunds, BBB F), most content paywalled, rigid |
| Calm | $14.99/mo, $69.99/yr, lifetime $399.99 | Best sleep stories in the category, polished | Price, sleep-heavy not meditation teaching, beginners lost in content ocean, non-refundable subs |
| Finch | Free generous; Plus $9.99/mo or $69.99/yr | Gentle gamified self-care pet, 4.9 stars, first app people actually keep | Dense UI at first, Plus is mostly cosmetic, Android pays more than iOS |
| Insight Timer | Free 100k+ meditations; Plus $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr | Unbeatable free library, community, live events, timer | Overwhelming choice, inconsistent quality, cluttered UI, pushy upsells |
| Daylio | Freemium, premium sub | 10-second mood logging, streaks, Year in Pixels, local-first privacy, 4.7 stars | 2026 trust collapse: lifetime buyers force-downgraded to subscription, restore failures, ads in paid tier |
| Breathwrk | Free limited; Pro $9/mo or $49/yr | Quick nervous-system results, structured breath programs, haptics | Thin free tier, Play rating 4.2 vs iOS 4.8, narrow scope |

## Ranked feature list

### Must-have (ship these or do not ship)
1. **Guided breathing by goal** (calm / sleep / focus / SOS) with visual + haptic pacing. The core stress-relief loop; Breathwrk proved 60-second sessions beat 20-minute meditations for retention.
2. **10-second mood check-in** (emoji scale + activity tags). Drives the daily habit and feeds every insight; Daylio/Finch retention engine.
3. **Stress score dashboard** combining check-ins with Health Connect (heart rate, sleep). Nobody in the pure-app space does Oura-style stress without hardware; this is the headline differentiator.
4. **Breathing programs and streaks** (7-day challenges, levels). Turns one-off sessions into a habit loop.
5. **Smart reminders** (exact alarms, quiet hours). Cheap to build, directly drives DAU.
6. **Journaling with guided prompts**. Reflection depth the mood check-in cannot give; low build cost.
7. **Sleep wind-down** (sound mixer + timer). Calm's most-loved category; must be present to compete.

### Should-have (v1.1 or premium drivers)
8. **AI companion chat** (reflective, CBT-flavored, with crisis disclaimer). Table stakes in 2026 after Headspace Ebb; keep wellness-not-therapy boundaries explicit.
9. **Insights dashboard** (mood vs sleep vs activities correlations, Year-in-Pixels view). Daylio's most beloved screen; the reason users stay for years.
10. **CBT exercise pack** (thought reframing, scheduled worry time, gratitude). Evidence-based content; cheap to author, high perceived value.
11. **Home-screen widget** (1-tap check-in, breathing shortcut, stress glance). Android strength; Glance is low cost.
12. **Gentle gamification** (streaks, milestones, original mascot-free reward system). Finch proved warmth beats punishment; do not clone the bird.
13. **Wear OS tile** for 60-second breathing from the watch. Medium cost, high delight.
14. **Local-first data with optional backup**. Privacy is a purchase reason now (FTC actions, Daylio's appeal); no account required.

### Nice-to-have (later)
15. Community groups and live events. Insight Timer's moat but heavy moderation cost; skip for v1.
16. Therapist directory / teletherapy. Different business, regulatory weight.
17. VR / biofeedback hardware integrations. Out of scope.

## Monetization recommendation

- **Freemium with a genuinely generous free tier.** Finch and Insight Timer prove free goodwill converts better long-term than a hard paywall on a wellness app. Core breathing library, mood check-ins, streaks, basic stats, and widget are free forever.
- **Premium: $4.99/month or $29.99/year, annual shown first.** Undercuts Headspace ($12.99) and Calm ($14.99); matches the journaling-app price norm and Charan's existing pricing logic.
- **Lifetime purchase $79-99, honored permanently.** Daylio's 2026 betrayal (lifetime buyers force-downgraded) created a trust gap; a real lifetime tier is a marketing weapon.
- **Paywall these:** unlimited AI companion, advanced insights/correlations, full CBT program library, premium sleep sound packs, wearable deep analytics.
- **3-day trial on monthly, annual preselected.** Same trial mechanics as the quit-vaping app.

## How a new app wins in 2026

1. **Android-first, colorful Material You design.** Every competitor is muted or iOS-flavored; Charan explicitly wants color and proper Android design. This alone differentiates screenshots.
2. **Stress score from phone data.** Oura/WHOOP own hardware stress tracking; no pure app does it well with Health Connect data. Own this.
3. **Honest pricing.** Generous free tier plus a real lifetime option, in a year when Daylio burned its most loyal buyers.
4. **Privacy as a feature.** Local-first, no account required, plain-English privacy policy. Direct contrast to Headspace's cloud data collection.
5. **AI companion done responsibly.** Reflective coaching with explicit wellness-not-therapy boundaries, matching 2026's sharper scrutiny of AI mental-health claims.
